# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-24_

FreeDataDiet does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

## What the app does with your data

* It reads file names, file sizes and dates on your device to show what takes up space.
* It reads the list of installed apps and their storage sizes to show cache and data usage.
* It saves your settings (filters, theme, favourite folders, excluded folders) on your device only.

All of this stays on your device.

## What the app does not do

* It has no internet permission. It cannot send anything anywhere.
* It has no analytics, no advertising and no accounts.
* It does not upload, copy or share your files.

## Permissions and why they are needed

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| All files access | To read the size of every folder and to delete files you select. |
| Usage access | To read how much storage each app uses (app, data and cache). |
| Query all packages | To list every installed app in the App Diet view. |

Files are only deleted when you select them and confirm.

## Backups

Android backup is switched off for this app, so your settings are not copied to cloud backups.
