FreeDataDiet is a fast, private disk usage and app-cache analyzer for Android. Find large files, clear bloated app caches, and remove exact duplicates with zero ads and zero tracking.

Features:
- Disk Explorer: Visual size bars, column sorting, pinch-to-zoom multiline filenames, and drag-to-select.
- App Diet: View installed apps sorted by total size, app data, and cache, and jump straight to system settings to clear cache.
- Duplicate Cleaner: Recursive subfolder grouping and two-pass SHA-256 duplicate detection.
- Built-In Media Preview: Hardware-accelerated video playback and unified photo/video swiping, plus native FileProvider intent opening for documents and audio.
- External Storage Support: Analyze internal storage, micro-SD cards, and USB OTG drives.
- 100% Private: Requests no internet permission, contains no ads, and collects no telemetry.
